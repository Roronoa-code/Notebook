# Notebook: notes for coding agents

Electron app for Windows (plus an Android phone app in `phone/`). The owner is a beginner, so explain in plain UK English.

## Layout
- `main/library.js` handles all saving: library.json (atomic write + `.bak`), imports (copy + size check), notes, boards, Bin, backup/restore (verified copies into new folders), export. Plain Node with no Electron.
- `main/merge.js` is the phone sync merge (the rules in `docs/SYNC.md`) and the check of what the phone sends. Pure functions.
- `main/sync-server.js` is the phone sync server: plain Node `http` (TCP 47821, next ports if busy) and `dgram` (UDP 47822 discovery), pairing codes, bearer tokens (only SHA-256 stored). Paired phones, `pcId` and `keepReady` live in `sync.json` in the app's userData, never in the library.
- `main/main.js` is the window, the `nb://notebook/` protocol (`/app/` = renderer, `/lib/` = current library, with range support for video seeking), IPC handlers, dialogs, the network block (the page itself can't reach the web), the sync server's lifecycle, the tray, `--background` start and the single-instance lock.
- `main/preload.js` is the only bridge (`window.nb`).
- `main/backup.js`: backup, restore and export (the Library methods call it).
- `main/errlog.js`: the error log (`<userData>/logs/errors.log`): console.error, uncaught errors, crashed processes, the window's script errors, and the phone's own log (`POST /api/log` after a sync; phone side `ErrorLog.java`). Read it first when something went wrong.
- `main/autobackup.js`: "Back up every week" (settings in `config.json` under `autoBackup`; keeps the newest three it made, never touches others).
- `main/features.js` registers the build-plan features. Track A: `recognise.js` (models: type, clothing colours, styles, fingerprints; plain Node), `recognise-worker.js` (runs it in a utility process), `scanner.js` (background queue; results in `item.ai`, fingerprints in `<library>/ai/embeddings.json`), `suggest.js` (suggested groups and matching sets; never changes items). Track B: `downloader.js` (yt-dlp and gallery-dl; the only file to touch when TikTok or Pinterest change), `links.js` (filing saved links, retry list in `library.links`), `pinterest.js` (the Pinterest panel, its own `persist:pinterest` session; `adfilter.js` takes promoted pins (and pins found to be AI-made) out of Pinterest's answers at the network, `aidetect.js` is the AI-picture check (two ONNX detectors in `D:\Notebook Tools\models`, run in the recognition process), `pinterest-preload.js` hides any left), `feed.js` (Ideas: the Pinterest-style feed per board and For you; asks Pinterest's own data routes with the panel's session, ranks by the library's fingerprints, pictures kept in userData/feed), `raw.js` (previews for camera RAW `.dng` photos from the JPEG inside), `dates.js` (when a photo or video was taken: EXIF, MP4 creation time, dated file names), `namer.js` + `namer-worker.js` (proper names from Qwen3-VL-2B q4 on the CPU, a separate low-priority process that closes when done; `naming.js` tidies its answers). The recognition worker closes after a minute idle (it holds several GB of GPU memory). DirectML can't run Qwen3-VL (MultiHeadAttention unsupported, and wrong output on the decoder), hence the CPU.
- Tools and models live outside the app in `D:\Notebook Tools` (`models/`, `bin/`), or `NOTEBOOK_TOOLS`. The phone's For you picture model (`PhoneVision.java`: CLIP ViT-L/14 as the 8-bit `vision_model_quantized.onnx`, the PC's model so fingerprints agree) is copied from there into the APK at build time (`phone/app/build.gradle.kts`, about 300 MB, never kept in the project) and run offline with ONNX Runtime. It fingerprints the library and fetched pins in the background (`ideas/vectors.bin`); `PhoneTaste.java` orders For you by look, by the sources' turns and by what Save / Not for me taught. Without the model file the app still builds and For you works without looking at pictures.
- `renderer/`: `smart.js` (filters, labels on an open item, style list, Suggested), `links.js` (paste-to-save, Links retry list, Pinterest panel), `app.js` (sidebar with add, search, boards, Bin, Phone and Library menu; the mood board grid, thumbnails, drag-drop, refresh after a phone sync), `ideas.js` (Saved | Ideas, the Ideas grid), `sort.js` (sort menu, month headings), `search.js` (words plus picture search: CLIP text vs the library's fingerprints; "my …" keeps to own items), `stacks.js` (fanned stacks: drag across to flick, drop a card on a card to stack, Ctrl-click picking), `viewer.js` (open item, note editor, Tidy up, Show on phone switch), `crop.js` (the crop frame; crops are shown with `object-view-box`, files never change), `previews.js` (thumbnails, three at a time), `shortcuts.js` (the ? sheet), `drag.js` (cards onto boards and cards, reordering boards), `phone.js` (the Phone panel), `util.js`, `styles.css`. Classic scripts, no build step. Fonts are bundled. Two tones: grainy background, one flat surface colour for panels.
- `phone/app/src/main/assets/www/`: the phone screens. `app.js` (state, screens, actions), `motion.js` (screen changes that open out of their button, the photo overlay, the name's pull-to-sync and long-press, the board shelf while a card is held, floating sheets, stacks and drag-to-stack), `pager.js` (tabs as one strip that follows the finger: Recent / Notes / For you, a board's Saved / Ideas), `fan.js` (the purple orb and its thumb-arc menu), `fluid.js` + `vendor/liquid.js` (the approved Liquid drop menu and gentle morph), `viewer.js` (in-place photo zoom, sideways paging, swipe-down and the details drawer), `item.js` (photo, video and note screen markup), `media.js`, `text.js`, `ideas.js` (For you and board Ideas), `sort.js` (sort sheet, month headings), `forms.js` (small forms), `sync.js` (the Sync screen), `mock.js` (stand-in for the Android side when testing on the PC).

## Rules
- Everything works offline except what the user asks to go online: saving from TikTok/Pinterest links (the downloader tools, run from the main process), the Pinterest panel (its own session) and Ideas (the main process asks Pinterest with the panel's session when Ideas are looked at; the page only sees pictures through `nb://notebook/feed/`). The page itself stays blocked from the web. Recognition must stay fully offline once the models are downloaded (`allowRemoteModels` off). No cloud storage, no uploads, no paid APIs, no Supabase. Phone sync stays on the home network (private addresses only), as set out in `docs/SYNC.md`. That file is the contract with the phone app: don't change behaviour without changing both.
- The user's corrections (`item.labels`) must never be overwritten by a re-scan; recognition only writes `item.ai`.
- Google Photos copies (`main/gphotos.js`, owner's request): after a Gallery trash batch, the phone reads each trashed file's SHA-1 and the Google Photos copies with that SHA-1 go to Google Photos' trash (restorable 60 days; restoring the batch restores them). It drives the PC's own Chrome/Edge with its own profile (`<userData>/google-photos`), signed in once in a normal window (Google refuses sign-in in Electron or under remote control), then headless over the DevTools protocol, using Google Photos' own web requests (`swbisb` find by hash, `XwAOJf` trash/restore). Nothing else in the account is read or changed.
- Imports always copy; the library and its Bin never change originals. The explicitly requested Phone Gallery feature is the only exception: a reviewed PC batch may trash or restore exact MediaStore items through Android system consent. Never permanently delete phone media or silently fall back to deletion. See `docs/GALLERY.md`.
- Phone motion clocks: a requestAnimationFrame time can be earlier than `performance.now()` taken during a long task (a slow library call). Every rAF loop clamps its elapsed time at 0 and springs are stepped in small slices; otherwise counts, the bottom surface and the tab strip overshoot (R05/R07/R08).
- The phone's bottom-right area (dock.js) is one surface: everything down there, including board options and a pin's sheet, comes out of the + (the owner's choice; audit R12 kept deliberately). What rides it is drawn whole, scaled to fit inside the surface; the old contents are gone by half way and the new appear from there.
- The keyboard's height reaches the page every frame from MainActivity (`nbKeyboard`); forms.js is its only owner.
- Dark mode only. Liquid glass, fun motion, but every element needs a purpose (no decorative dots or gradients).
- Don't change data shape in library.json without a migration. It's version 2 now (board `updatedAt`, `tombstones`), migrated from v1 on load. Changes that should sync must bump the item's `updatedAt`.

## Checks (run all before delivering)
The folder path contains `&`, which breaks `npm run`, so call node directly:
- `node scripts/test-library.js`: saving, boards, Bin, backup/restore, export, recovery, v1 to v2 migration and the sync merge rules.
- `node scripts/test-sync.js`: starts the real sync server on 127.0.0.1 against a temporary library and acts as the phone (pairing, sync, media up/down, discovery, address check).
- `node scripts/test-ui.js`: drives the real window (needs ffmpeg for sample media), including the Phone panel, hide-to-tray and `--background`. It sets `NOTEBOOK_USER_DATA`, `NOTEBOOK_SYNC_HOST=127.0.0.1` and `NOTEBOOK_SYNC_PORT` so it never uses your settings, never triggers Windows Firewall, and never registers Notebook to start with Windows.
- Phone screens (need Microsoft Edge; use the mock bridge in `phone/.../www/mock.js`, never the phone): `node scripts/test-phone-nav.js`, `node scripts/test-phone-media.js`, `node scripts/test-phone-library.js` (search, Bin, mood-board cards), `node scripts/test-phone-gestures.js` (swipe down to close, picking, stacks, sheets), `node scripts/test-phone-swipe.js` (tab swipe, board shelf, pull-down), `node scripts/test-phone-ideas.js` (For you, board Ideas, save, hide, open), `node scripts/test-phone-audit.js` (the UI/motion audit's acceptance checks: tab geometry, route continuity, header picture, Bin taps, Undo, status area, video failure). `node scripts/test-phone-recording.js` (the 30 Sept recording audit R01–R19/V01–V05, replayed with slow library calls as on the phone; `NB_ALL=1` reports every group, for comparing builds). `node scripts/test-phone-continuity.js` (the 1 Oct fix pack NBF-01–07: one picture while a pin flies home through saves and redraws, For you never swapping what is shown unasked, the picking bar out of the + with Stack dim and no empty shape, picked cards inside their own space; `NB_ALL=1` too). For you learning (`PhoneTaste.java`: Save and Not for me steer sources, words and colours): `phone/app/src/test/java/com/mani/notebook/PhoneTasteTest.java`, plain Java with the org.json jar from the Gradle cache (`javac -cp json.jar -d out PhoneTaste.java PhoneTasteTest.java`, then `java -cp json.jar;out com.mani.notebook.PhoneTasteTest`).
- Ideas without the internet: `test-features.js` uses a stand-in feed (`NOTEBOOK_FAKE_FEED=1`); `test-pinterest.js` checks real Ideas.
- `node scripts/test-pc-motion.js`: the PC's motion brought over from the phone (a binned card seen leaving, Undo growing it back, a pin's close-up flying from its pin, idea pictures only once loaded, New ideas beam + droplets). Second monitor, stand-in feed.
- `node scripts/test-adfilter.js`: the Pinterest ad filter on its own. `node scripts/audit-app.js`: screenshots of every screen with a realistic test library (for looking over the design).
- Build plan features: `node scripts/test-features.js` (recognition UI with a stand-in recogniser: filters, corrections surviving restart and re-scan, style list, suggestions; no models needed). With the models and tools in `D:\Notebook Tools`: `node scripts/test-recognition.js` (40-image accuracy, A1/A2 pass marks), `node scripts/eval-suggestions.js` (A3 groups and sets to rate), and with the internet `node scripts/test-links.js` (20 links, B1) and `node scripts/test-pinterest.js` (paste-to-save, retry list, Pinterest panel, B2). Test windows open on the second monitor (`NOTEBOOK_WINDOW_DISPLAY=second`).
- Build the installer: `node node_modules/electron-builder/cli.js --win nsis`. Then run the UI check against it with `NOTEBOOK_EXE="…\dist\win-unpacked\Notebook.exe"`.
